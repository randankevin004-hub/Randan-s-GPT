Randan's GPT

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Beta software

Randan's GPT is operated by Randan Kevin. It is designed to run AI models locally. This policy explains what stays on your PC and what the app sends to other services.

Stored on your PC

Chat history, memories, settings, profile details and app databases are stored under your Windows application data folder. Text or code files you choose to attach are read locally and included in a prompt sent to your local Ollama service. Sign-in refresh credentials are kept in Windows Credential Manager when available.

Information sent to services

  • Supabase: if account features are configured, your email, password during sign-in, display name and authentication/session data are sent to Supabase over HTTPS to provide account access. The app does not receive or store your password as a local plaintext file.
  • Ollama: prompts, conversation context and attached text are sent to the Ollama service running on your PC to generate responses. Models are downloaded from Ollama when you choose to install them.
  • GitHub: the app may request public release metadata to check whether a newer version is available. GitHub receives the request and your network address; no chat content, account data or files are included in this request.
  • Optional MCP tools: if you connect external tools, information needed for a tool call may be sent to that tool or service. Review its privacy terms and do not connect tools you do not trust. The app asks before tool calls when confirmation is enabled.

Analytics, sale and sharing

The app is not designed to upload chat history to Randan's GPT servers. It has no advertising or analytics service. The operator does not sell personal information. Third-party services process the information described above under their own policies.

Retention and your choices

Local data remains on your PC until you remove it. You can view and delete saved memories in Settings. Deleting the app does not necessarily remove its data folder; remove that folder yourself only after backing up anything you want to keep. Account data is managed by Supabase; to request account deletion or ask a privacy question, contact Randan Kevin on WhatsApp at +254 708 063 827.

Children and changes

The beta is not directed to children under 13. This policy may be updated as the app changes; the effective date above will be revised when that happens.

Applicable law

This policy is interpreted under the laws of Kenya, subject to privacy rights and protections that cannot legally be limited.

This short beta policy is general information, not legal advice. Have it reviewed for your specific circumstances before wider public distribution.
